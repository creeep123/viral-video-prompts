# One clear product shot

[Back to the library](../README.md#published-examples) · [Watch the product-film example](https://reelboop.com/blog/product-video-ad-prompts)

The public ReelBoop example shows ivory headphones above a stone surface. Watch how the silhouette reads against the background and how movement affects edge clarity. The linked guide shows the video as an existing product-film example.

**What to reuse.** Give one clip a single visual job. If you want to show finish, bring attention to a surface. If you want to show how a product fits into a space, hold the outline and give the viewer a clear reference for size. Add prices and calls to action during the edit.

For a product photo, contrast the first, middle, and final frames with the original. Compare proportions, marks, seams, shadows, and packaging text. Reduce the movement or use a wider reference if the render changes a detail that needs to stay accurate.
