# Blocking a duet so both roles read

[Back to the library](../README.md#published-examples) · [View the ReelBoop Bandit template and its public shot examples](https://reelboop.com/create/bandit-ai-trend)

ReelBoop’s published widescreen template shows how a 15-second duet can establish two roles before putting them in the same shot. Its public walkthrough identifies the lead on the lit lawn, the second performer in a tighter dry-grass shot, then both in a blue glass-block room.

**What to reuse.** Assign each performer a role before rendering. Give each a shot where their face is easy to identify, then use a shared frame to bring them together. Start from separate chest-up photos with space above the hair and both shoulders visible. When reframing for a vertical placement, check that the shared shot still holds both people.

For an original duet, use your own or authorized references and music. Build the role blocking into the storyboard, then review whether a viewer can distinguish the performers at normal speed.
