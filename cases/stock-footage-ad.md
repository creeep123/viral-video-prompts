# A stock-footage ad in 12 seconds

[Back to the library](../README.md#published-examples) · [Watch the finished ad and get the edit kit](https://reelboop.com/blog/how-to-make-a-video-ad-with-stock-footage)

ReelBoop assembled this fictional plumbing ad from licensed stock clips and an original end card. The recipe was rerun with FFmpeg from the public download and matched the published video. The file is 720 × 1280, 30 fps, 12 seconds, and silent.

| Timeline | What happens | Why it is there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:03 | A waterfall fills the frame. | The dramatic amount of water sets up the contrast. |
| 0:03–0:08 | Cut to a dripping faucet. | The viewer sees the actual problem in one shot. |
| 0:08–0:12 | Hold a plumbing booking card. | The ad closes on one action the viewer can take. |

**What to reuse.** Put the strongest visual contrast first, then quickly make its connection to the problem clear. Start with one call to action and test the export on a phone with sound off. Reframe landscape footage before export and verify that source audio is muted if the edit should be silent.

The exact recreation uses the FFmpeg kit. The article also offers a manual Clipchamp route based on Microsoft’s documentation. Footage sources are [Pavel Danilyuk’s waterfall](https://www.pexels.com/video/vertical-video-of-a-waterfalls-8859850/) and [Charlotte May’s faucet detail](https://www.pexels.com/video/a-footage-of-water-dripping-from-a-faucet-5823848/) under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/).
