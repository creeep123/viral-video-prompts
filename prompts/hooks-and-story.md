# Hooks and short stories

[Back to the library](../README.md#prompt-library)

Give each shot one job. Use documented product footage when the shot is evidence for a specific claim.

## Pattern break to proof

**Best for** — A short opener followed by a real detail. Generate the opener from text or choose suitable licensed stock.

```text
Vertical close-up of [unexpected visual] filling most of the first
frame. Show one clear motion: [action]. Hold the same composition
for the first beat, then finish on [matching shape or movement]
near [position in frame] so it can cut to the next shot.
```

**Edit.** Cut to a concrete object or action that shares the shape or movement. Use a real detail shot as proof when the video makes a product claim.

**Review** — Watch the first two shots silently. The second shot should quickly explain why the unusual first shot belongs.

## Reaction and reveal

**Best for** — A creator-led clip with a clear payoff. Build two shots from footage or reference images you have permission to use.

```text
Medium close-up of [performer] looking toward [side of frame].
One small reaction follows: [eyebrows lift, a smile forms, or a
brief pause]. Keep the camera locked with space in the direction
of the gaze. End with the reaction easy to read.
```

```text
Show [reveal object] clearly in the opening frame. It moves once
in [direction or action], then settles into a clear close-up.
Hold long enough to identify the object and the reason for the
performer's reaction.
```

**Check the edit.** Keep gaze direction consistent. If viewers need a caption to know where to look, shorten the setup and move the reveal earlier.

## Three-beat service ad

**Best for** — A 9:16 ad with one problem and one action. Mix footage and generated inserts in your editor.

```text
Plan a short vertical ad for [service] with one promise: [promise].
Opening shot: [a visual hook that relates to the service].
Middle shot: [the concrete problem the viewer can recognize].
Closing frame: clear background with room for a service name
and one action: [CTA].
Write one short overlay line per beat. Keep each shot focused on
one visible idea and add the final text in the editor.
```

**Check the export.** Watch the MP4 on a phone with sound off. Open the actual placement preview and check that its controls leave the copy visible. Confirm the click leads to the page promised by the CTA.
