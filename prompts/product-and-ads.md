# Product and ad shots

[Back to the library](../README.md#prompt-library)

Set the aspect ratio and duration in your tool. Start with an image you can use and a product with a clear outline. Add price, claims, and CTA text in the editor.

## Drawer reveal

**Best for** — A small object you want viewers to notice immediately. Image to video, 4–6 seconds to start.

**Input.** A reference photo showing a partially open shallow drawer, the product already visible, and enough room ahead for the drawer to move.

```text
Start from the supplied frame. The shallow drawer glides forward a short
distance until [product] is fully visible. The product stays in the same
position relative to the drawer. Use a locked three-quarter camera and
steady side light. Ease the drawer to a stop and hold a clear view of
the product in the last beat.
```

**Adapt it** — Swap in a drawer tray, a display box lid, or a sliding case only when the reference already shows the part that will move.

**Review** — Compare the first and last frames for changes in product shape and drawer geometry. If the open part deforms, shorten the move and start from a wider reference crop.

## Material light pass

**Best for** — Showing the finish on glass, lacquer, or metal. Image to video, 3–5 seconds to start.

**Input.** A close-up in soft light with a clearly visible surface and clean silhouette.

```text
Start on the supplied close-up of [product]. A soft strip of light
travels slowly across the visible [material] from left to right.
Keep the camera, product, seams, and background fixed. Keep the
overall exposure consistent. End with the reflection off the main
detail and hold the final view.
```

**Adapt it** — Name the surface you need to reveal, such as the brushed grain on the bottle cap, and use a reference that already shows it.

**Review** — Look at the edges and fixed seams as the reflection crosses them. If the surface starts bending or changing texture, use a softer light pass.

## Overhead layout reveal

**Best for** — A kit, ingredient set, or flat lay that needs space for a title. Image to video, 4–6 seconds to start.

**Input.** A straight-down photo with all objects visible and extra background around the layout.

```text
Begin on the supplied overhead photograph. Pull straight back a
short distance, keeping the camera perpendicular to the table.
Every object stays in its original position and orientation. Keep
the surface and shadows consistent. Finish on the wider layout
with open space on the [left or right] for a title added in the edit.
```

**Adapt it** — Set the title position before you choose the crop. If the uploader trims the reference to portrait, check the remaining space before rendering.

**Review** — Count the objects and compare their angles at the endpoints. Review the newly visible edges before adding type.
