# Transitions and loops

[Back to the library](../README.md#prompt-library)

For a two-shot transition, render the shots separately. Lock a shared framing cue, then set the cut in the editor.

## Doorway match cut

**Best for** — Moving between two rooms, times of day, or set designs. Two image-to-video shots, 3–4 seconds each.

**Input.** Two images with the doorframe at the same place and roughly the same scale.

**Shot A**

```text
Use reference A. The camera advances slowly toward the doorway.
Keep the vertical edges of the frame parallel and the opening
centered. End just as the near edge passes the lens and the
doorway fills the frame.
```

**Shot B**

```text
Use reference B. Begin with the doorway centered and filling the
frame. Continue the same slow forward movement into [new room].
Keep the camera level and bring [first object in room] into view
before easing to a stop.
```

**Check the cut.** Trim both clips to where the same edge crosses the same point. If the geometry jumps, cut while the near edge covers the largest area.

## Foreground wipe

**Best for** — Cutting from one product or setting to another. Two short shots made with your own images or footage.

```text
Start on [subject A] in a locked portrait frame. A solid [color]
panel in the foreground slides from left to right. It covers the
whole frame by the last beat, with even light across the panel.
```

```text
Start with the same evenly lit [color] panel covering the frame.
It continues moving left to right until [subject B] is revealed.
Keep the camera locked. Hold the revealed subject for a beat.
```

**Check the cut.** Match panel color, direction, and apparent speed. Trim to the fullest frame coverage or place a matching single-color frame between the clips.

## Return-to-start loop

**Best for** — A small motion that can repeat in a feed or landing page. Image to video, about 4 seconds to start.

**Input.** A locked composition with one moving object, such as a ribbon tail beside a still product.

```text
Begin from the supplied still. With the background and camera
fixed, [small object] moves gently outward, slows, and returns
toward its starting position. Keep light and shadows steady.
Hold the last pose briefly near the original composition.
```

**Check the loop.** Compare the first and last silhouettes, then watch three repeats in the editor. Trim off an extra hold if the loop pauses at the seam.
